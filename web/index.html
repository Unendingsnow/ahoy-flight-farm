How the drip works

01 · Fixed budget

The farm can only ever pay out what has actually been deposited into it. The total is capped — there is no mint function behind it.

02 · Pro-rata, per second

Every staked NFT is one equal share. Fewer NFTs staked means a bigger slice each — the total stream never changes.

03 · Your exact NFTs

Specific token IDs go in and the identical IDs come back. The farm verifies delivery on the way in, so a 404 reroll can never swap your rare.

04 · Always exit-able

Withdrawing and claiming can never be paused or blocked by the owner. Your NFTs are yours on demand.
